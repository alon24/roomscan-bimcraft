1. Put a known object on the same flat surface
A bank card works well. Lay it flat next to the thing you want to measure and take the photo from straight above.
×
2. Check the green corners are on the reference
Drag a green corner to fix it. Then tap two points to measure between them: slide your finger to place it exactly, lift to set it. Only things lying flat on the same surface as the reference are measured correctly.
3. Measurements

    Typical accuracy is about 1% when the photo is taken straight above. Everything runs on your own server; nothing leaves it.
